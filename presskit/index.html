About the game

Studio Sim is a strange art game created by a painter. You work in a big, empty white studio, making paintings by layering and positioning stickers: wonky prefab patterns and shapes that you rotate, scale and stack until something abstract takes shape.

When a painting is done, you ship it down the freight elevator. Finished work hangs in your gallery, visitors come to react to it, and each painting you ship raises your standing in the art world.

It's a slow, hands-on, slightly absurd game about the feeling of making things, and about the odd social world that grows up around art.

Features

  • Paint with stickersLayer, rotate and scale wonky patterns and shapes into abstract paintings.
  • Freight elevator shippingSend finished canvases down to your gallery and grow your reputation.
  • Visitors with opinionsPeople wander your gallery and react to what you made.
  • A real studioModeled on an actual painter's studio, with a moving sun and day-night light.
  • Steam extrasAchievements, leaderboards and Steam Cloud saves.
